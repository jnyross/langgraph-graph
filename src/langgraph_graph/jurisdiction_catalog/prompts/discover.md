# Additional candidate discovery

Propose only jurisdictions not already in the supplied candidate list that
appear to have both Meta consumer services and independent law-making or
regulatory authority over privacy, competition, youth safety, intellectual
property, or accessibility. Focus on newly relevant supranational bodies and
subnational units with their own platform or privacy statutes. Do not propose
places solely because they are administrative regions. Return a structured
`candidates` list with `name`, supported `level`, optional `parent_id`,
`domains_priority`, and an evidence-grounded `rationale`. Every proposal must
still undergo the normal verification step.
